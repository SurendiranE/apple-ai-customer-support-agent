# Decision Log

1. **Selected AppleSupport** — keeps the agent focused on one brand and support voice.
2. **Six intents** — compact taxonomy avoids sparse classes.
3. **250-example golden set** — within the assignment's required range.
4. **Manual label review** — prevents generated keyword labels from being treated as ground truth.
5. **Separate intent and handling** — problem type and operational risk are different predictions.
6. **Two few-shot examples per intent** — gives the LLM demonstrations while holding them out of scoring.
7. **TF-IDF retrieval** — deterministic and easy to reproduce without a vector database.
8. **Top-three retrieval** — balances context with prompt size.
9. **Sensitive-intent escalation** — account, payment and refund cases may need human/account-specific handling.
10. **0.60 confidence threshold** — low-confidence cases are escalated.
11. **Majority baseline** — reveals the effect of class imbalance.
12. **TF-IDF + Naive Bayes baseline** — provides a conventional offline ML reference.
13. **Separate reply evaluation** — classification metrics do not measure response quality.
14. **Human validation of LLM judge** — judge scores require evidence of agreement.
15. **No fabricated LLM metrics** — API-dependent results are generated by the evaluation command.
